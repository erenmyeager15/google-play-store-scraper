# Google Play App Metadata & Ratings Scraper Promotion Notes

## Positioning

Promote this as a direct package-ID lookup for factual public Google Play app metadata and aggregate metrics.

Best-fit users:

- ASO teams with a known competitor app list
- App-market researchers monitoring ratings, installs, versions, and pricing
- Product teams comparing Android app metadata across storefronts
- Automation builders that need clean package-level records

Do not advertise keyword search, category browsing, descriptions, media, individual reviews, or user identity data. Those features are outside this Actor's current scope.

## Suggested Demo

1. Open the Actor.
2. Enter `com.openai.chatgpt`.
3. Keep `maxResults: 1` and proxy disabled.
4. Run the Actor.
5. Show developer, aggregate rating counts, installs, price, version, and official URL.
6. Open `RUN_STATUS` to show the validated outcome and counters.

Built a direct Google Play app metadata scraper on Apify.

Input:
- Android package IDs or official app URLs
- country and language

Output:
- app name and developer
- aggregate rating and review counts
- installs and price
- version and Android requirement
- official Google Play URL

The quick test uses one package ID, requires no login or API key, and returns machine-readable run diagnostics.
