# Google Play US Android Agency Research — Promotion Notes

## Positioning

Promote a bounded Android competitor snapshot for US app agencies, ASO consultants and product teams with a known package list. Lead with a dated client research table, official app links and CSV exports; do not imply access to exact downloads, revenue or US audience demographics.

Best-fit users:

- ASO teams with a known competitor app list
- App-market researchers monitoring ratings, installs, versions, and pricing
- Product teams comparing Android app metadata across storefronts
- Automation builders that need clean package-level records

Do not advertise keyword search, category browsing, descriptions, media, individual reviews, or user identity data. Those features are outside this Actor's current scope.

## US Agency Starter

1. Open the Actor.
2. Open [US Android App Competitor Research](https://apify.com/fascinating_lentil/google-play-store-scraper/examples/us-android-competitor-research).
3. Keep the illustrative Notion, Evernote and OneNote packages, or substitute a client's app and two competitors.
4. Use US/English settings, three results and no proxy. Explain that this is a configuration, not newly verified cloud output.
5. If a permitted run is made, check `RUN_STATUS` before showing aggregate ratings, install brackets, versions, prices and official URLs.
6. Export all fields to CSV. Save dated snapshots and follow the [comparison guide](https://github.com/erenmyeager15/google-play-store-scraper/blob/main/examples/US-ANDROID-COMPARISON-GUIDE.md). Comparisons are downstream; the Actor does not generate history or alerts.

The original one-ChatGPT quick test, runtime defaults and pricing stay unchanged. Do not make a paid demo run, schedule, pricing change or extra build merely to promote this package.
